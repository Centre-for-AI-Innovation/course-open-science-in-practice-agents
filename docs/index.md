# Purpose of the course
The purpose of this course is to enable doctoral students to develop a critical, informed, and practical understanding of agentic AI tools — autonomous AI systems capable of planning and executing multi-step tasks — as they are increasingly deployed in scientific research workflows.

As these tools become integrated into how research is designed, conducted, and reported, it is essential that researchers can use them responsibly, evaluate their limitations, and maintain scientific integrity.

# Intended learning outcomes
At the end of the course, the student is expected to be able to:

- Explain the concept of agentic AI, including how agentic systems differ from simple generative AI tools, and describe the types of tasks they can and cannot reliably perform in a research context
- Critically evaluate the output of agentic AI tools for accuracy, reproducibility, and potential bias, using domain-relevant examples
- Apply agentic AI tools to a defined research task (e.g. literature synthesis, code generation, data wrangling, or manuscript drafting) while maintaining a transparent and auditable workflow
- Design an AI-assisted research workflow that complies with open science principles, including appropriate documentation, version control, and disclosure practices
- Discuss the ethical responsibilities of researchers who use agentic AI, including questions of authorship, accountability, data privacy, and research integrity
- Formulate guidelines or recommendations for responsible AI use appropriate to their own research field or group
